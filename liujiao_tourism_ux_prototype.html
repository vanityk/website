Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know when planning a trip to Liujiao Township.

Liujiao is located adjacent to Chiayi HSR Station. You can take Chiayi County Bus No. 7307 or 7308 directly to Suantou Sugar Factory in about 10–15 minutes, or take a convenient taxi ride (approx. NT$150–200). Bicycle rentals and YouBike 2.0 stations are also available nearby.
Most nature and heritage sites (such as the Puzi River Bike Trail and Tanqian Historic Courtyards) are free of charge. Suantou Sugar Train rides cost approximately NT$100–150. A full day including local lunch, shaved ice, and bike rental generally costs between NT$300 to NT$600 per person.
Yes! Bike rental shops are conveniently located right at the entrance of Suantou Sugar Factory. Tandem bikes, regular road bikes, and electric bikes are available for hourly or whole-day rental.
Do not miss Suantou Sugar Factory’s red bean ice cream with condensed milk, traditional Liujiao sesame biscuits, and the famous mutton noodles and duck soup along the old village market streets.
